# desd-repo
## Overview:
This project aims to develop a marketplace connecting local food producers with customers, enabling producers to list and manage their products. The system validates product information before publication and supports external verification of any organic certifications. Producers can update product quantities and seasonal availability, while automatic stock management ensures products become unavailable when out of stock and can become available again when replenished, depending on their availability period.
