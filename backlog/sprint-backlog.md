Sprint 1 backlog:

1. **Analyse of Use Case**
  - 1.1 - Read and analyse the use case: Identify the main business problem and the purpose of the marketplace functionality.
  - 1.2 - Identify the stakeholders: Identify the parties involved, including producers, customers and relevant external services.
  - 1.3 - Identify the system's objectives: Establish what the marketplace needs to achieve through the functionality described.
  - 1.4 - Identify the producer's activities: List the activities performed by a producer when listing and managing products.
  - 1.5 - Identify the system's activities: Identify the actions the system performs, including validating information, publishing products and updating                                                 availability.
  - 1.6 - Identify external system interactions: Identify how the marketplace interacts with the external or simulated organic certification verification                                                     service.
  - 1.7 - Identify exceptional scenarios: Identify what happens when information is invalid, mandatory fields are missing or the verification service is                                               unavailable.
  - 1.8 - Document the findings: Summarise the use case analysis for use in subsequent modelling and functionality scoping tasks.

2. **Analysis of Major Business Processes**
  - 2.1 - Identify the major business processes: Identify the main processes supported by the marketplace, using the case study as the basis.
  - 2.2 - Define the purpose of each process: Describe what each identified process achieves for the enterprise.
  - 2.3 - Identify activities within each process: Break down each major process into its main business activities.
  - 2.4 - Identify process inputs: Determine the information or events required for each process to begin.
  - 2.5 - Identify process outputs: Establish the outcomes or information produced by each process.
  - 2.6 - Identify relationships between processes: Determine how the processes depend on or interact with one another.
  - 2.7 - Document the business processes: Compile the processes, activities, inputs, outputs and relationships into a structured overview.

3. **Analysis of Organisational Responsibilities**
  - 3.1 - Identify organisational actors: Identify the people, organisations and external services involved in the business processes.
  - 3.2 - Define actor responsibilities: Establish the responsibilities of each identified actor.
  - 3.3 - Assign actors to business activities: Determine which actors perform each activity within the identified processes.
  - 3.4 - Identify decision-making responsibilities: Establish who makes decisions at relevant points in the processes.
  - 3.5 - Identify interactions between actors: Determine where actors exchange information or depend on one another.
  - 3.6 - Document organisational responsibilities: Compile the actors, responsibilities and interactions into a structured overview.

4. **Create a Strategic BPMN Model**
  - 4.1 - Identify the groups of systems and participants working together: Identify the relevant organisational participants, marketplace system and external services.
  - 4.2 - Identify the processes to include: Select the major business processes identified in Task 2 for representation in the model.
  - 4.3 - Identify the tasks needed within each process: Identify the major activities that must be represented in each business process.
  - 4.4 - Identify the actors responsible for each task: Establish which participants perform the identified activities.
  - 4.5 - Identify the starting events: Determine what initiates each major business process.
  - 4.6 - Identify the sequence of activities: Establish the order in which the activities occur.
  - 4.7 - Identify decision points: Identify decisions that affect the direction of a business process.
  - 4.8 - Identify interactions between participants: Establish where participants exchange information or communicate during a process.
  - 4.9 - Identify process boundaries: Determine where each process begins and ends and how it relates to the wider enterprise.
  - 4.10 - Select appropriate BPMN elements: Determine which BPMN elements are needed to represent participants, activities, events, decisions and interactions.
  - 4.11 - Draw the initial BPMN model: Construct the diagram using the identified processes, participants and relationships.
  - 4.12 - Review BPMN notation: Check that the appropriate BPMN symbols and connections have been used.
  - 4.13 - Validate the process flows: Check that the activities, decisions and interactions follow a logical sequence.
  - 4.14 - Review the model against the case study: Confirm that the model accurately reflects the enterprise and its business processes.
  - 4.15 - Finalise the strategic BPMN model: Incorporate agreed corrections and prepare the model for the Sprint 1 deliverables.
