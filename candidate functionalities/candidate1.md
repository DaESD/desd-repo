**Major Candidate Functionality 1:**
Product Listing Creation and Publication:

This functionality covers the process through which a registered producer submits a new product to the marketplace and makes it available to customers. 
It includes the management of product information, validation of submitted details and verification of organic certification claims where applicable.
The purpose of this functionality is to ensure that product listings contain valid and reliable information before being published. 
It also addresses unsuccessful submissions and external verification failures to prevent incomplete or unverified listings from being published incorrectly.

**In scope**
- Submission of new product listings by registered producers.
- Collection and management of product information, including name, description, category, price, quantity, farm origin and availability period.
- Handling of additional information, such as harvest dates, allergens, best-before dates and organic certification.
- Validation of mandatory information and identification of invalid or missing details.
- Allowing producers to correct errors and resubmit product information.
- Verification of organic certification through an external or simulated service.
- Handling rejected certification claims and temporary verification service failures.
- Publication of products after the required validation and verification checks have succeeded.
- Making successfully published listings visible to customers.

**Out of scope**
- Registration of new producer accounts and account administration.
- Processing customer orders, payments, refunds or cancellations.
- Issuing organic certifications or managing certification applications.
- Delivery, distribution and fulfilment of products.
- Product advertising, promotional campaigns and personalised recommendations.
- Advanced product analytics and sales reporting.

**Scope boundary**
This functionality begins when an already registered producer submits a new product listing and ends when the product has either been successfully published or remains unpublished because validation or certification verification has not been completed successfully. 
It is concerned with establishing a valid marketplace listing rather than managing the product's ongoing stock and seasonal availability after publication
