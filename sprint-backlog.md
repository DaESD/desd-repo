Sprint 1 backlog:

1. Analyse the use case - Identify the key features of the system in the case study and the relevant system interactions.
2. Identify major business processes - Identify the main processes and how they relate to one another across the enterprise.
3. Identify organisational responsibilities - Define which actors are responsible for activities and decisions in each major process.
4. Create strategic BPMN model - Model the enterprise's high-level business processes, key actors, activities, decisions and interactions.
5. Identify candidate functionality 1 - Define Producer Product Listing and Inventory Management, including its purpose, boundaries and main business value.
6. Identify candidate functionality 2 - Select and scope a second major functionality appropriate to the marketplace.
7. Develop initial product backlog - Translate both candidate functionalities into prioritised user stories with initial acceptance criteria.
